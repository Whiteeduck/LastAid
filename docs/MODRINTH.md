# Description

**Last Aid** turns Minecraft health into a survival medical system. It is built on **First Aid** and heavily inspired by **Project Zomboid** and **The Long Dark**.

Your body is split into parts. Wounds bleed, arrows get stuck, bones break, frostbite eats your fingers, and a dirty bandage can kill you two days later. Press **H** to open the body panel: it shows every condition and lets you treat it.

## What's inside

**Bleeding**
- Four stages: scratch, laceration, deep wound, arterial bleeding. An artery kills in under a minute.
- Rags, plasters and bandages stop light bleeding. Deep wounds need a **sewing kit**. Arteries need a **tourniquet** right away, but take it off within 15 minutes or the limb dies.
- Arrows stay in the wound until you pull them out.
- Dressings get dirty. Take them off, wash them in water, put on a clean one.
- Blood stains on the ground, blood trails behind crawling players.
- Anemia after heavy blood loss.

**Infection**
- From dirty dressings, bites, untreated open fractures and gangrene.
- Fever, weakness, then sepsis. Antibiotics, one a day. Medical alcohol helps prevent it.
- Sepsis from a limb kills the limb: amputate it or die.

**Bones**
- Hairline, closed and open fractures, dislocations. Splints, or the bone heals wrong.
- Both legs broken: you crawl. Splints and morphine: you limp.

**Cold** (with Cold Sweat and Primal Winter)
- Hypothermia, common cold, frostbite per limb, gangrene and amputation with a surgical knife.

**And more**
- Concussions, pain, painkillers and morphine, overdoses with antidotes.
- Carry or drag a wounded friend.
- If both arms are gone, the player holds food in their teeth.
- Create recipes for medicine (vanilla fallbacks for alcohol and antibiotics).

## Requirements
- NeoForge 1.21.1
- Optional: Cold Sweat, Primal Winter, Create
- **Do not install together with the original First Aid**: Last Aid already contains it.
